## 42.4 Summary

The main concepts and results of this chapter are listed below: $\bullet$  Quadratic optimization problems; quadratic functions. $\bullet$  Symmetric positive definite and positive semidefinite matrices. $\bullet$  The positive semidefinite cone ordering. $\bullet$  Existence of a global minimum when $A$ is symmetric positive definite. $\bullet$  Constrained quadratic optimization problems. $\bullet$  Lagrange multipliers; Lagrangian. $\bullet$  Primal and dual problems. $\bullet$  Quadratic optimization problems: the case of a symmetric invertible matrix $A. \bullet$  Quadratic optimization problems: the general case of a symmetric matrix $A. \bullet$  Adding linear constraints of the form $C^{\top}x = 0. \bullet$  Adding affine constraints of the form $C^{\top}x = t$, with $t \neq  0. \bullet$  Maximizing a quadratic function over the unit sphere. $\bullet$  Maximizing a quadratic function over an ellipsoid. $\bullet$  Maximizing a Hermitian quadratic form. $\bullet$  Adding linear constraints of the form $C^{\top}x = 0. \bullet$  Adding affine constraints of the form $N^{\top} x = t$, with $t \neq  0$.
