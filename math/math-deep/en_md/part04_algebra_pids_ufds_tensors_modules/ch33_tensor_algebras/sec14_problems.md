Problem 33.2. Given two linear maps $f$ : $E \to$ E' and $g: F \to$ F', we defined the unique linear map
$$f \otimes g: E \otimes F \to E'\otimes F'$$
by
$$(f \otimes g)(u \otimes v) = f(u) \otimes g(v),$$
for all $u \in E$ and all $v \in F$. See Proposition 33.9. Thus $f \otimes g \in$ Hom(E $\otimes F$, E' $\otimes$ F'). If we denote the tensor product $E \otimes F$ by T(E, $F)$, and we assume that $E$, E' and $F$, F' are finite dimensional, pick bases and show that the map induced by $f \otimes g \mapsto$ T(f, $g)$ is an isomorphism
$$\sim$$
$$Hom(E, F) \otimes Hom(E', F')= Hom(E \otimes F, E'\otimes F').$$
