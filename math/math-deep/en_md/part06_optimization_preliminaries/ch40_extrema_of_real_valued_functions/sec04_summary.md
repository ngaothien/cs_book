# Summary

The considerations in this chapter reveal the need to find methods for finding the zeros of the derivative map
$$dJ : \Omega \to E',$$
where $\Omega$ is some open subset of a normed vector space $E$ and E' is the space of all continuous linear forms on $E$ (a subspace of $E^{*})$. Generalizations of Newton’s method yield such methods and they are the object of the next chapter.

## 40.4 Summary

The main concepts and results of this chapter are listed below: $\bullet$  Local minimum, local maximum, local extremum, strict local minimum, strict local maximum. $\bullet$  Necessary condition for a local extremum involving the derivative; critical point. $\bullet$  Local minimum with respect to a subset $U$, local maximum with respect to a subset $U$, local extremum with respect to a subset $U. \bullet$  Constrained local extremum. $\bullet$  Necessary condition for a constrained extremum. $\bullet$  Necessary condition for a constrained extremum in terms of Lagrange multipliers. $\bullet$  Lagrangian. $\bullet$  Critical points of a Lagrangian. $\bullet$  Necessary condition of an unconstrained local minimum involving the second-order derivative. $\bullet$  Sufficient condition for a local minimum involving the second-order derivative. $\bullet  A$ sufficient condition involving nondegenerate critical points. $\bullet$  Convex sets, convex functions, concave functions, strictly convex functions, strictly concave functions. $\bullet$  Necessary condition for a local minimum on a convex set involving the derivative. $\bullet$  Convexity of a function involving a condition on its first derivative. $\bullet$  Convexity of a function involving a condition on its second derivative. $\bullet$  Minima of convex functions on convex sets.
