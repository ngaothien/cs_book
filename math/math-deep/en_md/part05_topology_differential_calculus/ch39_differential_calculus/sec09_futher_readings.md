## 39.9 Futher Readings

A thorough treatment of differential calculus can be found in Munkres $[134]$, Lang $[116]$, Schwartz $[157]$, Cartan $[34]$, and Avez $[9]$. The techniques of differential calculus have many applications, especially to the geometry of curves and surfaces and to differential geometry in general. For this, we recommend do Carmo $[53, 54]$ (two beautiful classics on the subject), Kreyszig $[109]$, Stoker $[172]$, Gray $[84]$, Berger and Gostiaux $[13]$, Milnor $[130]$, Lang $[114]$, Warner $[192]$ and Choquet-Bruhat $[38]$.
