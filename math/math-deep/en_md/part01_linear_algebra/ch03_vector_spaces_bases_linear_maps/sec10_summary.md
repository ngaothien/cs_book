## 3.10 Summary

The main concepts and results of this chapter are listed below: $\bullet$  The notion of a vector space. $\bullet$  Families of vectors. $\bullet$  Linear combinations of vectors; linear dependence and linear independence of a family of vectors. $\bullet$  Linear subspaces. $\bullet$  Spanning (or generating) family; generators, finitely generated subspace; basis of a subspace. $\bullet$  Every linearly independent family can be extended to a basis (Theorem 3.8). $\bullet  A$ family $B$ of vectors is a basis iff it is a maximal linearly independent family iff it is a minimal generating family (Proposition 3.9). $\bullet$  The replacement lemma (Proposition 3.11). $\bullet$  Any two bases in a finitely generated vector space $E$ have the same number of elements; this is the dimension of $E$ (Theorem 3.12). $\bullet$  Hyperplanes. $\bullet$  Every vector has a unique representation over a basis (in terms of its coordinates). $\bullet$  Matrices $\bullet$  Column vectors, row vectors. $\bullet$  Matrix operations: addition, scalar multiplication, multiplication. $\bullet$  The vector space $M_{m,n}(K)$ of $m \times  n$ matrices over the field $K$; The ring $M_{n}(K)$ of $n \times  n$ matrices over the field $K$.
