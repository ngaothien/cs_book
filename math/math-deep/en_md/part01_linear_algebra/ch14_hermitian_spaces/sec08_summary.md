## 14.8 Summary

The main concepts and results of this chapter are listed below: $\bullet$  Semilinear maps. $\bullet$  Sesquilinear forms; Hermitian forms. $\bullet$  Quadratic form associated with a sesquilinear form. $\bullet$  Polarization identities. $\bullet$  Positive and positive definite Hermitian forms; pre-Hilbert spaces, Hermitian spaces. $\bullet$  Gram matrix associated with a Hermitian product. $\bullet$  The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and the Minkowski inequality. $\bullet$  Hermitian inner product, Hermitian norm. $\bullet$  The parallelogram law. $\bullet$  The musical isomorphisms $\flat$ : $\overline{E} \to E^{*}$ and $\sharp$ : $E^{*} \to \overline{E}$; Theorem 14.6 $(E$ is finite-dimensional). $\bullet$  The adjoint of a linear map (with respect to a Hermitian inner product). $\bullet$  Existence of orthonormal bases in a Hermitian space (Proposition 14.11). $\bullet$  Gram–Schmidt orthonormalization procedure. $\bullet$  Linear isometries (unitary transformations). $\bullet$  The unitary group, unitary matrices. $\bullet$  The unitary group U(n). $\bullet$  The special unitary group SU(n). $\bullet$  QR-Decomposition for arbitrary complex matrices. $\bullet$  The Hadamard inequality for complex matrices. $\bullet$  The Hadamard inequality for Hermitian positive semidefinite matrices. $\bullet$  Orthogonal projections and involutions; orthogonal reflections. $\bullet$  Dual norms. $\bullet$  Nuclear norm (also called trace norm). $\bullet$  Matrix completion.
