## 24.3 Chasles’s Identity

$$\longrightarrow  \longrightarrow  \longrightarrow$$
Given any three points a, $b, c \in E$, since $c = a +$ ac, $b = a +$ ab, and $c = b +$ bc, we get
$$\longrightarrow  \longrightarrow  \longrightarrow \longrightarrow  \longrightarrow$$
$$c = b +bc = (a +ab) +bc = a + (ab +bc)$$
by (A2), and thus, by (A3),
$$\longrightarrow  \longrightarrow  \longrightarrow$$
$$ab +bc =ac,$$
which is known as Chasles’s identity, and illustrated in Figure 24.8.
